## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Mostly authored for this product, but the copy stops short of asserting its own stated differentiator, and the surface has drifted from the design spec it was just documented against. The Fraunces roman/italic pairing ("Hold the places / *you love.*") is a genuine, disciplined signature move most competitors wouldn't bother executing correctly, and the honest proof stats ("35 sculptures made," not an inflated vanity number) fit a made-to-order brand. But PRODUCT.md Principle 2 says explicitly: "lead with the combined relief-plus-buildings differentiator; don't let copy collapse it into generic custom map art." The homepage does exactly that — the word "buildings" appears exactly once on the entire page, inside a customer testimonial, never in the brand's own voice. ## Overall Impression

The typographic system is doing real, disciplined work — the roman/italic Fraunces pairing is the strongest thing on the page. But the page doesn't yet say the one thing that makes this product different (relief *and* buildings, not just terrain), and its own design system's central discipline — "terracotta marks one thing, never a fill" — is broken by the page's own promo band and an off-brand green metric color. The biggest opportunity is tightening copy around the stated differentiator and reining in accent-color sprawl back to the rule DESIGN.md itself just set.

## What's Working

1. **The roman/italic Fraunces pairing is executed with real discipline.** "Hold the places / *you love.*" and "From map to *masterpiece*" use the exact dimmed-italic-clause pattern DESIGN.md specifies, never a second color or weight substituting for it.
2. **Proof stats stay honest rather than inflating** — "35 sculptures made / ↑ 3 this week" and "100% UK made" read as small-batch credibility, fitting a made-to-order brand better than a fabricated large number would.
3. **Mobile touch-target discipline is real, not decorative** — a 44px minimum height is enforced site-wide under the 640px breakpoint, and layout space is reserved for async-loaded prices to prevent layout shift.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The stated differentiator (relief + real buildings) never appears in the site's own voice.**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md calls this the entire competitive wedge against flat map-art sellers, yet the word "buildings" appears exactly once on the page — inside a testimonial, never in brand copy. Visitors have no way to learn the actual product from the site itself.
- **Fix**: Rewrite step 02's body copy ("Our studio translates contours and elevations into a crisp, physical relief") to name both relief *and* buildings explicitly; consider working it into the hero subhead too.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Terracotta's "One Marker Rule" is broken in multiple places, most severely as a full section background.**
- **Why it matters**: DESIGN.md states terracotta is "never a background fill... never used as decoration for its own sake," yet `#custom-band` paints an entire section solid terracotta. `.proof-trend` uses bright green (`#4ade80`) — a second saturated accent DESIGN.md explicitly forbids, and one that reads like a SaaS growth widget against the artisan "Field Notebook" tone. The products section alone repeats accent color across eyebrow, CTA, active-thumb border, badge, and four "Customise →" links.
- **Fix**: Recolor `#custom-band` to ink or paper-deep with a single terracotta CTA; change `.proof-trend` off green; reduce accent-colored elements in the products section to one deliberate marker.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] Meta description and OG/Twitter tags claim "any place on Earth," contradicting UK-only scope everywhere else.**
- **Why it matters**: A live, shareable artifact (search snippets, social cards) overpromises fulfillment scope, risking confused non-UK visitors clicking through from a shared link.
- **Fix**: Update `<meta name="description">`, `og:description`, `twitter:description` to say "UK locations," matching the hero copy and schema.org entries that already get this right.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Hero product image is entirely hidden on mobile.**
- **Why it matters**: `.hero-right{display:none}` under 900px removes the product photo on every phone. For a tactile physical object, the photo is the pitch — mobile visitors get dark text with no visual evidence of what they're buying.
- **Fix**: Replace the side-by-side grid with a stacked or cropped image treatment on mobile instead of removing the image outright.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Four different CTA verbs point to the same destination.**
- **Why it matters**: "Design yours," "Customise yours →," "Make this place yours," and "Customise →" (×4) all resolve to `/store/`. A first-time visitor may reasonably wonder if these are different flows, adding friction right at the decision moment.
- **Fix**: Standardize on one CTA verb across nav, hero, and gallery/size-strip.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`
